1. The Core Operational Intelligence Loop

1. SEE

Live GIS & Stations

Real CAAQMS ground station network + Open-Meteo live API. Hotspot clustering around traffic junctions.

2. UNDERSTAND

Source Attribution

Chemical Mass Balance (CMB): Vehicles (48%), Dust (21%), Industry (17%), Weather Inversion (9%).

3. PREDICT

Multi-Horizon AI

Predictions from +15m to +6h with diurnal planetary boundary layer physics and atmospheric confidence bounds.

4. ACT

Decision Engine

Policy actuation: Signal synchronization, commercial freight diversion, and mist cannon dust suppression.

5. MEASURE

ECO Simulator

What-if counterfactual sandbox quantifying Before vs. After AQI, PM2.5 % drop, avoided kg/day, and citizens protected.
